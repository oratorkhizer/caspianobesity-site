Why this course

Obesity is the disease under the diseases you already treat.

Diabetes, hypertension, fatty liver, sleep apnoea and PCOS mostly share one root cause: excess visceral fat. Treat the weight and you help all of them at once. Yet Asian-Indian patients develop these problems at a lower BMI, and most of us were never formally taught to diagnose, stage and treat obesity itself. The GLP-1 and dual GIP/GLP-1 era has changed what is possible faster than any textbook we trained on. This course closes that gap in one focused year.

The CASPIAN framework

Classify – stage the disease, not just the BMI.
A

S

P

I
