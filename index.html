Why this course

Obesity is the disease under the diseases you already treat.

Diabetes, hypertension, fatty liver, sleep apnoea, PCOS, most share extra visceral fat as a root cause. Treat the weight and you help all of them at once. But Asian-Indian patients get these problems at a lower BMI, and most training never taught you how to treat obesity itself.

  • The GLP-1 and dual GIP/GLP-1 era changed what's possible, and what patients ask for.
  • Every cut-off, dose and script here is tuned for the Indian "thin-fat" phenotype.
  • You leave each session with something you can use in clinic the next morning.

"No one chooses obesity. Patients try hard, and their own body pushes back. Our job is to change that fight."

Questions

Good to know before you register.

Who is this course for?

Registered physicians (MBBS and above) who see people with obesity, diabetes or metabolic disease and want a structured, modern way to treat weight, GPs, physicians, diabetologists, endocrinologists and specialists building an obesity service.

Do I need prior training in obesity medicine?

No. The course starts from the science and builds to prescribing and practice. If you already treat diabetes, you'll move quickly.

How much time does it take?

One 3-hour in-person session a month for 12 months, in Hyderabad, plus short pre/post quizzes. It's designed around a practising clinician's schedule.

What do I walk away with?

The full skill set of the CASPIAN framework, the course textbook and reference tools, and a certificate on passing the final exam.

What does it cost?

The founding-batch fee is ₹2,999 (from ₹9,999) for the full 12-module programme, the textbook and the certificate, founding pricing for the first cohort only. Pay securely online to reserve your seat.

What if it isn't right for me?

There's a money-back guarantee: a full refund any time before Session 1, and if the first session isn't value for money, a full refund within 7 days of it. After that, one free deferral to the next batch and one free exam retake.

Is this an accredited course, and is it a degree or diploma?

On passing the final exam you receive the CASPIAN Certificate in Obesity Medicine from Caspian Healthcare Foundation, Hyderabad. Accreditation of the programme by a national professional body in obesity and diabetes is under process, and CME credit points will be applied for from the Telangana State Medical Council once accreditation is granted. This is an independent educational certificate, not a university degree, diploma or fellowship.

How do I secure a seat?

Seats are reserved on payment. Pay the ₹2,999 founding fee securely online and your seat is confirmed straight away, places are capped at 40. The enquiry form and WhatsApp (+91 89784 54547) are for questions and don't hold a seat.

How much attendance is required?

You need to attend at least 80% of the live modules, that's 10 of the 12 sessions in person. If you have to miss a session (up to two), you keep your place by watching the recording and completing that module's post-session assessment within 14 days. Miss more than two live sessions and we'll help you defer the outstanding modules to the next cohort.

What are the assessments and feedback forms?

Each module has a short pre-session and post-session assessment, and a feedback form after every module so we can keep the teaching sharp. After Module 12 there's a 60-question final exam. To be certified you need ≥80% attendance, all twelve module assessments and feedback forms completed, and a pass (60%; 75% for distinction) on the final exam.

What if I attend but skip an assessment or feedback form?

That module is marked incomplete until the assessment and feedback are submitted. You can complete any outstanding ones before you sit the final exam. The certificate is issued once attendance, all assessments and feedback, and the final exam are complete, so nothing is left hanging.

Is the certificate verifiable?

Yes. Every CASPIAN certificate carries a unique Credential ID and a QR code. Anyone can confirm it's genuine at caspianobesity.com/verify, scan the QR or enter the ID to see the holder's name, the credential and the issue date.
