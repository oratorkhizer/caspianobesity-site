About

Who runs the CASPIAN Certificate in Obesity Medicine.

The CASPIAN Certificate in Obesity Medicine is a practical certificate course that teaches registered physicians to treat obesity in the GLP-1 era, tuned for Indian practice. It runs over twelve monthly in-person modules in Hyderabad, built around the CASPIAN clinical framework (Classify, Assess, Screen, Personalise, Intervene, Anchor, Nurture), with a course textbook, assessments and a certificate on completion.

Who operates this course

The course is offered by Caspian Healthcare Foundation (CHF), a registered public charitable trust, and is directed by Dr Khizer Hussain Junaidy, MD — diabetologist and obesity specialist, SCOPE-certified obesity physician (World Obesity Federation).

Our approach

The course is funded entirely by participants' tuition, with no pharmaceutical or commercial sponsorship of content, faculty or assessment. It is case-based and clinic-ready, designed so that doctors can use what they learn the same week.
